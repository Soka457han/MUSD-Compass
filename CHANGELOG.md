# Changelog

## Wave 2f: gauge staking, redemption warning, previews, proof page, Bitcoin wallets (11 Nov 2026)
- Stake in Mezo's gauge from `/vault` (mainnet only): stake sMUSD, unstake, claim MEZO. The guard allows four more calls on mainnet and nothing else: sMUSD `approve` (to the gauge only, exact amount, capped at 2,500), gauge `deposit` (capped), `withdraw`, and `getReward` for the caller's own account. Checked on Mezo mainnet with read-only `eth_call` on 11 Nov 2026: the gauge's staking token is sMUSD and its reward token is MEZO; `deposit` needs the approval first; `withdraw` passes for a staker; `getReward(account)` passes only for the account itself.
- Redemption warning: when MUSD trades under the redemption floor on Mezo's MUSD/mUSDC pool and a trove is in the front quarter of the redemption line, the trove page, `/vault` and the `/watch` alerts say so with both prices. The alert needs a 0.002 margin to start and clears at the floor, so a price sitting on the floor cannot repeat it every 15 minutes. The Borrow → Earn dialog on mainnet adds the same fact.
- Before and after: the top-up and repay boxes show the ratio after the change; the mainnet dialog adds the place in the redemption line now and after, from `GET /api/trove/{address}?addBtc=` or `?repayMusd=` (new `preview`, `market` and `nicr` fields).
- `/proof`: every transaction Compass has sent, with explorer links, from `docs/evidence.md`. The mainnet list is empty until the first real mainnet transaction.
- Bitcoin wallets, view only: Unisat, OKX (Bitcoin) and Xverse can be picked on `/vault`. Compass works out the wallet's Mezo account (the OrangeKit Safe address, checked against the factory's `predictAddresses` on Mezo mainnet and testnet, 11 Nov 2026) and shows its position. No action buttons: Bitcoin-wallet accounts act in the Mezo app. Mezo Passport itself was tried and left out: it needs React 18, is GPL-3.0, and adds several hundred packages.
- No wallet needed to look (12 Nov): for any looked-up address, `/vault` shows the Actions and gauge sections with that address's real numbers and every control off; acting still needs the connected wallet to own the address. The trove page always states the MUSD price against the redemption floor, and has a "What if you add BTC or repay MUSD?" box that answers from the live trove with no wallet. The wallet picker names the network the page is on.
- Review fixes: a cleared pressure alert is always reported; a failed price or queue read no longer sends a false all clear; `sendSafely` refuses to send when the wallet's chain is unknown; the network choice is locked while a wallet is connecting.

## Wave 2e: mainnet actions (10 Nov 2026)
- Savings Vault on Mezo mainnet: with `NEXT_PUBLIC_MAINNET_WRITES=1`, `/vault` has a network choice at the top and reads, deposits, withdraws and claims on the chosen network. Deposits and withdrawals on mainnet wait behind the typed-amount dialog. `GET /api/vault/{address}?network=mainnet` reads the mainnet vault. The flag stays empty in the repo, so production is unchanged until the owner sets it.
- Hard cap: the guard refuses a mainnet deposit, MUSD approval or new-trove debt above 2,500 MUSD per transaction (`MAINNET_CAP_MUSD` in `web/src/lib/sendSafely.ts`), before any simulation. It also refuses zero or non-numeric amounts on both networks. Top-up, repay, withdraw and claim are not capped.
- Borrow → Earn, top-up and repay were already built for both networks; they now follow the page's network choice. Checked against Mezo mainnet with read-only `eth_call` on 10 Nov 2026 (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-10-mainnet-actions.md`): `openTrove` with HintHelpers hints, `addColl`, `repayMUSD`, vault `withdraw` and `claimYield` all pass simulation.
- With the flag on: the Savings Vault card, the trove page and the `/watch` alerts link to `/vault?net=mainnet`; the FAQ says real funds can be used. With the flag off nothing changes, and `?net=mainnet` is ignored.
- Fixed: a slow read for one network could land after switching to the other; only the newest read fills the page now. A send sequence cannot start twice.
- "Try to break it" has a fourth button: a 1,000,000 MUSD mainnet deposit, refused by the cap.
- Tests: guard matrix (every contract and function pair on mainnet, exactly seven pass), cap edges, and `web/e2e/mainnet.spec.ts` with a stand-in wallet and RPC (runs with `MAINNET_E2E=1` against a flag-on build).
